## A mistake I made and corrected inside this round

Removing `forecolor` from the rule styles — to stop the double-draw — made the purple rule under
the logo render **black**, because JasperReports then strokes the rectangle with its default pen.
`forecolor = backcolor` is correct for a rule; the strip is only right for the band **fills**,
where a stroke in the fill colour hides the border that should be black. Caught on the next
render and put back.

Two further bugs the guards caught: my `<detail>` band pattern matched a **comment** containing
the text `<detail>`, so it swept in title-band elements and reported a false overflow; and after
rewriting the border loop to cover every cell in a row I had dropped the "already present" test,
so a second run added duplicate borders to R10.029 and R10.034. Both files were restored from
backup and re-applied once.
